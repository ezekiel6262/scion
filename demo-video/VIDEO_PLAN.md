# Scion demo video plan

Target: 3–4 minutes, 1920×1080, American male neural narration.

1. **Problem and product — Landing**: trading intelligence lacks privacy, proof, attribution and safe execution.
2. **Network and lineage — Home**: public/sealed strategies, performance context, forks and unmistakable Demo labels.
3. **Accountable calls — Plays**: entry, target, stop, stance, timestamp and reveal state.
4. **Complete live moment — Markets & swap**: search a token, obtain a live 1Click quote, approve in the user wallet, verify the settled receipt.
5. **Bounded autonomy — Trading agents**: owner policy, dedicated NEAR account, KMS-isolated key, deterministic policy, 1Click execution, pause/revoke.
6. **Security and NEAR role — Docs**: encrypted production storage, scoped capabilities, verified checkout splits, and honest sandbox labels.

The published cut must use the live product frames in `frames/`, narration in `narration.txt`, and subtitles generated alongside the audio.
