Four steps, start to finish.

No custody, no bridging by hand, no trust required in the person on the other side.

1

Publish

Buy into a strategy now, or publish tokens you already hold with a signed holding proof. Choose public or sealed.

2

Unlock

Followers buy one key for one section, or read public strategies free. Keys bind to their wallet and expire.

3

Fork

They change the allocation, set their own limits and execute from their own wallet through NEAR Intents.

4

Earn

Every fork pays its original, every improver who gets accepted earns a share, and every royalty-share NFT gets its cut.

Proof, not promises.

Every number on Scion traces back to something signed.

Records from receipts

Returns come from solver-signed receipts chained together, and the gateway signs the stats. Positions and timing never leave.

Keys that can’t be shared

Each key is a signed capability bound to the buyer’s wallet key. Widen its scope and the signature breaks.

Calls that can’t be backdated

A play’s seal is public from the moment it is posted, so a reveal can always be checked against it.
